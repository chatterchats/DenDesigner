# Arcade & Halloween sample credits

This development sample adapts existing ZComArcade and Plague Night assets into static props.
It includes no playable arcade engine, original Nintendo screen/banner art or embossed logos.

- Cabinet model: “Vintage Nintendo arcade cabinet”, identified as free to use in ZComArcade's
  existing credits. Original creator/source attribution is not recorded there; confirm that
  provenance before publishing this sample as an independently redistributed asset pack.
- Cabinet palette, attract-screen art and marquee adaptation: Chats. The marquee contains the
  Star Wars Zero Company logo, a Lucasfilm trademark.
- Pumpkin and jack-o’-lantern: SEspider, *Halloween 2k11 Scene*, Blend Swap 26793,
  https://blendswap.com/blend/26793, CC-BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.
  Adaptations by Chats: scene extraction, reduced geometry, orientation/scale normalization,
  rind/flesh/stem materials, and conversion from the runtime meshes into static mesh assets.
- Game/engine material parents are referenced from the installed game; their asset files are
  not distributed in the sample.

Original asset licenses remain in force. This is a local development sample, not a clearance
of third-party art for public distribution.
